Why Momentum Matters

Momentum is the tendency of a trend to continue once it has started.

Traders measure momentum with indicators such as the relative strength index, which compares recent gains with recent losses.

A reading above seventy is often called overbought, and a reading below thirty is often called oversold.

What the evidence says

Studies across many markets find that past winners keep winning for several months before the effect fades.

The effect is strongest in small companies and weakest in large ones.1
